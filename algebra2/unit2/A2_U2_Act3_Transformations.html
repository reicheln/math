Activity

Read any graph in four steps

Strategy card
  1. Shape → parent. V is |x|, U is x², a half-curve from a starting point is √x.
  2. Vertex or starting point → (h, k). Here (1, −2): x − 1 inside, − 2 outside.
  3. Pattern → a. Over 1 from the vertex: up ½. (Over 2: up 1.) So a = ½. For √x use over 4 = 2a.
  4. Opens down? → negative a. This one opens up, so a stays positive.

y = ½|x − 1| − 2 — vertical compression by ½, right 1, down 2.

Word Bank for the Quiz

parent function
The simplest function in a family: y = |x|, y = x², y = √x.
translation
A slide. Same shape, new place.
vertical translation
Up or down: + k outside.
horizontal translation
Left or right: x − h inside. The sign looks opposite.
vertex
The point of the V or the bottom or top of the U.
starting point
Where the √x graph begins.
vertex form
y = a·f(x − h) + k. (h, k) is the vertex or starting point.
vertical stretch
a > 1 (ignoring the sign): taller, skinnier.
vertical compression
a between 0 and 1 (ignoring the sign): shorter, wider.
reflection across the x-axis
Negative a: the graph flips upside down.
domain
All the x-values. |x| and x²: all real numbers. √x: x ≥ h.
range
All the y-values. y ≥ k, or y ≤ k when reflected.
