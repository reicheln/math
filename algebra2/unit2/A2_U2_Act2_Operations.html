Operations Relay (pods, 30 min)

Operations Relay
One Chromebook per pod. 6 rounds × 4 cards: sum, difference, product, quotient.
Open

Roles — they rotate every card

Driver

The player on the screen's Whose turn box. Reads the card out loud, works it on scratch paper, taps the answer.

Checker

The next player. Checks the Driver's work before anything is tapped. Watch the signs.

Everyone else

Work the same card on your own scratch paper. Be ready to take over.
  1. Pick 3 players or 4 players on the left. The screen tracks whose turn it is.
  2. Each round shows f(x) and g(x). Do the cards in order: sum → difference → product → quotient.
  3. Part A: pick the simplified new function. Part B: type its value at the given x (a fraction like −3/5 is fine).
  4. Wrong answer? Read the hint and try again. First try = 2 points, after a hint = 1 point.
  5. When a card is done, pass the Chromebook to the next Driver. With 3 players, card 4 goes back to Player 1.
  6. After Round 6, type your pod name and class period. One person pastes the pod code and score into Classroom.
Difference cards: the minus sign goes to every term of g(x). Quotient cards: leave it as a fraction unless the top factors.
